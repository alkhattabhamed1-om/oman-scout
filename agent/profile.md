## Details for the agent

- Nationality: Omani.
- Studies: full-time Bachelor's student at a university or college in Oman. Age 21 to 24.
- Interests: technology and software, including AI. Open to any business idea that could be built as software.
- Participation: can enter competitions alone, or in a team formed with classmates.
- Does not have: a registered company or commercial registration (CR), a startup with revenue or customers, professional work history, or a graduate degree yet.
- Travel: happy to attend ordinary in-person events elsewhere in the GCC (for example the UAE). Not planning long or expensive trips, so far-away in-person events are for information only. Online events anywhere are fine.
- Language: reads English and Arabic.

### Eligibility rules for this person

- Competitions and programmes that need a registered company, revenue, or professional experience are "ineligible".
- School-only, postgraduate-only, graduate-only, executive-only or invitation-only events are "ineligible".
- Programmes limited to citizens or residents of another country are "ineligible".
- Age limits count: a limit that excludes ages 21 to 24 makes the item "ineligible".
- If an age cap, residency rule or student requirement is unclear, use "check" and say what is unclear.
- Paid professional conferences with no student price are "check", not "ineligible".
